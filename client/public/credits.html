James Strong, The Exhaustive Concordance of the Bible (1890)

Word lookup’s entry for each Strong’s number: the Hebrew or Greek word, its transliteration and pronunciation, and its renderings in the King James Version. Strong’s definitions are not used; meanings come from Morrish’s dictionary.

Licence: Public domain

Open Scriptures, Strong’s dictionaries in XML

Licence: Strong’s text public domain; the app’s file shared alike under CC BY-SA 4.0

Only Strong’s own fields are taken (data/strongs.json in the source); the Hebrew file’s Brown-Driver-Briggs outlines and TWOT numbers are not.
